Our story

One wood oven.
A stubborn belief.

Ember & Oak started in 2016 with a single oak-fired oven and a simple idea: that fire, given time and good ingredients, does most of the talking. Ten years on, the oven is bigger, the dining room is fuller — but the rule hasn't changed. If it can't be cooked over wood, it doesn't go on the menu.

We buy from eleven family farms within a hundred miles, butcher in-house, bake our sourdough every morning, and let the seasons write the menu four times a year.

"Gas is convenient. Wood has a memory. Every dish that leaves our pass carries a little of the oak it was born over."

— Mara Ellison, Chef & Founder
